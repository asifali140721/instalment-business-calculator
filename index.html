Pehle Din se Lazmi System

Hisaab jitna hi zaroori — warna collection rukti hai aur nuqsan tumhara hota hai, bank ki qist nahi rukti.

  • 2 guarantors (zamanat dene wale) har customer ke liye — CNIC copies ke sath.
  • CNIC + income proof verify karo — nokri / karobar ka saboot baghair phone mat do.
  • Bank ke samne business ko “mobile retail / trading” likho — stock khareed kar aage bechna. “Qarz dene ka business” mat likho, us ke liye alag licence chahiye hota hai.
  • Likhit agreement har sale par — total qeemat, down payment, qist ki date aur late / default par kya hoga, sab pehle se tay.
  • Har mahine pehle bank ki qist alag rakho — collection ka paisa pehle qist ke liye, munafa baad mein.
Risk wali baat: Customer qist rok de to phone wapas lena aur paisa wasool karna asal mushkil hai. Isi liye down payment zyada aur guarantors mazboot rakhna — upar Default wala slider badha kar dekho ke munafa kitni jaldi khatam hota hai.

Ye calculator andazon par hai — asal supplier rates, bank ki final qist aur approval bank hi tay karega. Loan ke liye maujooda notified hisaab: Tier 3 Rs 75 lakh tak 7% par, Tier 4 (Rs 75 lakh se Rs 2.5 crore) 8% par confirm hai, lekin Tier 4 ke named sectors mein mobile retail shamil nahi — bank se pehle confirm karna.
